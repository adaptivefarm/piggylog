# Health interventions

Append-only log of everything given to a pig: shots, vaccines, dewormers, treatments.
Newest at the bottom. Never edit a past entry — append a correction below it.

Every entry here also gets a one-line cross-reference in that day's log under
`## Health`, so the day reads complete on its own.

Record for each: **what** was given, **how much**, **how** (injection, in feed, in
water), **which animals**, **who** gave it, and a **link to the daily log**.
