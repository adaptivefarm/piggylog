# Health protocols

The playbook: what this farm does routinely, and what to watch for.
[`interventions.md`](interventions.md) is the separate, append-only record of what was
actually done. **Plan here, log there.**

This file is edited in place as the farm's practice changes.

---

## Set this up with the farmer

This starter ships with the headings and nothing under them, on purpose. A protocol
copied from the internet is worse than no protocol — it will be wrong for this farm's
climate, breed, and vet. Fill each section with what the farmer actually does, or leave
it empty and marked `TBD`.

**Where routine health advice should come from:** the farmer's own vet, the municipal
or provincial agriculture office, or the local Agricultural Extension Worker. Not from
an AI assistant, and not from this file. An agent's job here is to write down the plan
and hold the farm to it — not to design it.
